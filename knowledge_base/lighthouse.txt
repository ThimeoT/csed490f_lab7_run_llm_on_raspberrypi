The Skerrvik Lighthouse

The Skerrvik Lighthouse stands on a small rocky island of the same name, off the northern coast of the fictional kingdom of Valdoria. It was built in 1887 and is 42 metres tall, with 211 steps leading to the lantern room. Its lamp makes one full rotation every 14 seconds and its beam can be seen from 31 kilometres away.

The current keeper is Maren Aldous, who took over in 2009 from her uncle Tobias. Maren lives in the keeper's cottage with her cat, Pilchard, who is famous locally for refusing to climb more than 40 steps.

In fog, the lighthouse uses a foghorn that the islanders call Old Bertha. It sounds twice every 90 seconds. The supply boat, the Gannet, reaches the island every second Thursday, weather permitting. In the winter of 2019 the island was cut off for 23 days after a storm damaged the jetty.
