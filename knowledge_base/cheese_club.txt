Tuesday Knights Chess Club

The Tuesday Knights Chess Club meets every Tuesday at 19:00 in the Dunmere public library, room 4. It has 27 members and was founded in 1994 by the retired postman Oswin Pratt, who is still its president. Membership costs 35 euros per year, or 15 euros for students.

Each October the club organises the Pawn Storm Cup, a rapid tournament with an entry fee of 8 euros. Games last 15 minutes per player. The 2025 winner was Leila Haddad, who beat the club treasurer Pavel Novak in the final. The trophy is a gilded wooden rook nicknamed Gerald, which is kept in a glass cabinet at the library entrance.

The club's youngest member is 9 years old, and its oldest is 83. Beginners can join a free coaching session on the first Tuesday of every month.
