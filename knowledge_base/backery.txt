Crumb & Compass Bakery

Crumb & Compass is a small bakery in Reykjavik, founded in 2019 by Elin Sigurdsson. It opens at 5:40 every morning except Monday, when it is closed. The bakery bakes about 120 loaves a day in a wood-fired oven nicknamed Hekla.

Its signature product is the Aurora loaf, a sourdough bread made with a rhubarb-based starter. The starter, called Rufus, is fed every evening with water heated to exactly 38 degrees Celsius by a geothermal pipe. The Aurora loaf costs 1,450 Icelandic krona and sells out by 9:30 on most days.

The bakery employs six people. Its head baker is Jon Hallgrimsson, who previously worked in a pastry school in Lyon. On the first Sunday of each month, the bakery runs a free bread-making workshop for children, limited to 12 participants.
